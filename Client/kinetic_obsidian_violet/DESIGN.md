## Brand & Style

This design system channels the uncompromising craft, surgical precision, and fluid velocity seen in industry-defining tools like Linear, Apple, and Raycast. Built for enterprise leave orchestration and team availability, it reframes absence management from bureaucratic administrative overhead into a strategic, dignified pulse of team health.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Atmospheric Precision:** High-contrast modernist surfaces pairing deep cinematic obsidian night containers with crystalline, high-clarity daylight cards.
- **Micro-tactility:** Ultra-fine 1px hairline structural frames, subtle inner glow falloffs, and diffused chromatic shadows that mimic natural photonic reflection.
- **Editorial Data Presentation:** Massive, confident metric figures sitting alongside quiet, structured monoline typography and balanced metadata grids.
- **Emotional Intent:** Instills absolute confidence, executive calm, friction-free operation, and premium engineering rigor.

## Colors

The palette balances clean porcelain day surfaces with deep midnight architectural balance anchors and an ethereal violet spectrum.

### Surface Hierarchy & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4F5FA` (Light cool lavender-grey) provides an airy, anti-glare foundation distinct from clinical neutral greys.
- **Primary Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) reserved for tactical work cards, elevated sheets, tooltips, and interactive inputs.
- **Hero Balance Surface:** `#171A35` (Deep midnight navy) delivers authoritative focus for executive balance summaries, PTO allowance vaults, and priority headers.
- **Hero Ambient Glow:** Radial falloff using `#7464F5` at 18% to 28% opacity positioned behind hero balance counters.

### Functional & Accent Roles
- **Primary Action Accent:** `#7464F5` (Electric violet) drives decisive calls-to-action, focus indicators, timeline badges, and primary selection handles.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Accent:** `#A69BFF` (Soft periwinkle) handles secondary toggles, progress meter fills, subtle pill states, and data visualization overlays.
- **Hairline Borders & Structural Partitions:** `#ECECF3` (Ultra-subtle lavender-grey) renders razor-sharp internal gridlines without visual friction.

### Text & Semantic Scales
- **Text Primary:** `#191A25` (Deep charcoal) provides optimum contrast and readability across daytime surfaces. On dark surfaces (`#171A35`), text primary shifts to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Secondary / Metadata:** `#858797` (Muted slate-grey) anchors secondary copy, field headers, and inactive states. On dark surfaces, it renders as `#9A9CB8`.
- **Success / Approval:** `#27C383` (Muted emerald) for approved requests and positive allowance accruals.
- **Warning / Pending:** `#F5A623` (Warm amber) for leaves awaiting managerial clearance.
- **Critical / Overlap Alert:** `#FF5A76` (Electric rose) for coverage conflict warnings.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, fine-tuned through strict geometric proportions, deliberate negative tracking on display figures, and optical alignment.

### Typographic Principles
- **The Metric Monolith (`display-stat`):** The 120px figure acts as an architectural sculpture in the dark hero surface. Tracking is tightened to `-0.04em` with proportional numerals enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) for clean horizontal lockups.
- **Editorial Sub-headings:** Section titles and card identifiers employ `headline-md` and `headline-sm` with slight negative tracking to reflect modern Silicon Valley desktop application density.
- **Micro Metadata (`label-caps`):** Small meta tags, policy indicators, and column table anchors use uppercase styling at 11px with `+0.08em` tracking to preserve legibility without competing with primary data.
- **Text Alignment Rhythm:** When massive stats sit near secondary metadata, maintain a solid baseline alignment with tabular numbers for leave balances (e.g., remaining days, pending hours).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine balances generous architectural breathing room with the high-density information requirements of enterprise resource allocation.

### Layout Blueprint & Grid Structure
- **Canvas Container:** Centered layout capped at an ultra-wide max-width of `1440px` with an edge margin of `margin` (`2.5rem / 40px`) on desktop.
- **Columns & Proportions:** A 12-column fluid grid system connected by a standard `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutter.
  - **Executive Split:** Hero metrics and primary calendar visualization map across an 8-column primary block alongside a 4-column side pane reserved for team coverage lists and impending leaves.
  - **Compact Mobile Grid:** Collapses dynamically below `768px` to a single column with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and vertical card stack sequences.

### Spacing Philosophy
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight component-level architecture: badge paddings, button icon-to-text offsets, and avatar overlaps.
- `space-md` (16px) establishes internal card gaps and form stack cadences.
- `space-lg` (24px) establishes the internal padding of standard porcelain workspace containers.
- `space-xl` (40px) establishes standard section breaks and hero dark-card interior padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using precise physical light modeling, tinted luminous penumbras, and glassmorphic micro-borders.

### Layer Stack & Shadow Tokens

#### Level 0: Recessed & Canvas
- Ground level (`#F4F5FA`) with zero elevation. Recessed tracking strips (e.g., historical timeline tracks, inactive calendar dates) use an inner hairline shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(25, 26, 37, 0.04)`.

#### Level 1: Daytime Porcelain Card
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Structural Framing: Solid `1px` border in `#ECECF3`
- Shadow Stack:
  - `0 1px 3px rgba(23, 26, 53, 0.02)`
  - `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.04)`
  Provides a faint violet-tinted ground separation without muddy grey halos.

#### Level 2: Dark Midnight Hero Surface
- Background: `#171A35`
- Structural Framing: `1px` border with `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(166, 155, 255, 0.25) 0%, rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.05) 100%)`
- Ambient Core: Inner diffused radial gradient radiating from the stat center: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 30%, rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.22) 0%, rgba(23, 26, 53, 0) 70%)`
- Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 26, 53, 0.25), 0 0 80px -20px rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.15)`

#### Level 3: Floating Overlay & Flyout Drawers
- Applied to leave approval modals, command bars (`Cmd+K`), and date range flyouts.
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- Border: `1px solid rgba(236, 236, 243, 0.8)`
- Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(25, 26, 37, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(25, 26, 37, 0.04)`

#### Electric Violet Interactive Glow
- For primary action states and selected dates:
- Button Shadow: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.38), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.2)`
- Hover Elevation: Increases y-offset and expands ambient blur: `0 8px 24px 0 rgba(116, 100, 245, 0.48)`

## Shapes

The geometry pairs generous roundedness on structural shells with controlled pill-like radii on internal micro-controls.

### Geometric Scale
- **Structural Master Shells (Cards & Containers):** Fixed at `22px` border radius (`rounded-xl` equivalent). This high-radius treatment mimics luxury mobile operating system widgets and softens high-density enterprise tabular information.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Tuned to a refined `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg` equivalent) on standard sizes, or full continuous pills (`9999px`) on status chips and filter tags.
- **Form Inputs & Dialog Controls:** Uniform `12px` border radius, creating visual cohesion when inputs are nested inside `22px` cards.
- **Avatar Nodes & Team Indicators:** Standardized to absolute circular silhouettes (`rounded-full`).
